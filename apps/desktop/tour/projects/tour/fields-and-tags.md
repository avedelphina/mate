# Fields and tags

The block of lines at the top of this file is its *front matter*. Other tools read it too. Mate
shows it as a row above the page.

## Try it

- Change `owner` in the row. Press Enter, and the line in the file changes.
- Press **+ Property**. The list says what each field does, and offers the fields other documents of
  this context already use.
- `due` takes a date picker. A field comes out with its × button.
- **Tags…** in the bar sets the tags. A slash nests them: `tour/fields` is under `tour`.
- Once a document has tags, the rail can show your files under them. Press **Tags** above the files.

Mate changes one line at a time. It keeps your comments, your order and the rest of the block.

# Read and comment

Select any words on this page. A small bar appears with **Comment**, **Rewrite** and **Remove**.

- **Comment** leaves a note on the passage.
- **Rewrite** suggests a replacement. The comment gets an **Apply** button, and the text changes
  only when you press it.
- **Remove** suggests cutting the passage.

The threads sit in the margin, under **Review**, with their replies.

## Private until you share

A comment you leave is kept in the context, in a hidden folder, and not in this file. Nobody who
gets this document sees it. When you share it, Mate writes it into the file as an HTML comment
under the passage. Look at the line under the sentence above in a text editor, and you will see
the one that came with the tour. It lives in git with the sentence it points at.

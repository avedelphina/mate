# Drafts and the shelf

The shelf, in `shelf/`, is what is true now. [[how-a-context-works]] is on it.

A document in a project is a proposal. This project keeps its documents where they lie. Another
project may keep them in a `draft/` folder, and that works the same.

## Try it

1. Open this page's bar and press **Publish…**.
2. A dialog of the system asks you to confirm, and says what it replaces. It is the one place where
   something becomes true, so Mate does not let the window press it for you.
3. Cancel, unless you want a copy on the shelf. You can publish and then delete the tour.

When a document replaces one on the shelf, the old text is kept as history in `shelf/.history/`.

## Status

A document can say it is final with `status: final` in its front matter, as [[start-here]] does.
The bar shows **Final**, and **Publish…** is offered as the next step. It is a note to yourself and
to colleagues. It does not change what is true. Only the shelf does.

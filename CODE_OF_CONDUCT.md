# Code of Conduct

Mate follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text applies to everyone who takes part in this project: in issues,
pull requests, discussions, and other spaces the project uses.

## Summary

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be specific and patient with people who are learning.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or publishing someone's private
  information without consent.
- Keep discussion on the project. Say when something is outside its scope.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub: contact the
maintainer through the profile of the repository owner at
<https://github.com/avedelphina>, or use GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/github-community-guidelines)
form for content in the project. Reports are read by the maintainers and
handled confidentially.

Maintainers will follow the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant when they respond to a report.
